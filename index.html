How pricing works

  • Compute units. 1 CU is about one GPU-second on a reference card. Each job type costs a base amount plus some CU per 1,000 rows, scaled by column count. CPU pools pay 1.5× on GPU-heavy work.
  • Price. Compute (CU × the provider's rate) + agent fees + human review, then × surge, then a 10% platform fee. Minimum fare is $0.50.
  • Surge. Starts once the pool is more than 60% busy: ×1 + 2.5 × (busy − 60%), capped at ×2.5. The on-device route never surges.
  • Matching. Offers are ranked by price and ETA, weighted by your slider, nudged by rating and preferred providers. Privacy routes are hard filters.
  • Payouts. Providers earn compute × surge. Agents earn their fees × surge.
